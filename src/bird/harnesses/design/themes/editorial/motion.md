# Editorial — motion

Restrained, print-like: motion on arrival only, never while reading.

- Page load: masthead and lede fade-in 500 ms, ease-out, stagger 80 ms, ≤3 elements.
- Links: underline offset/thickness 150 ms; no color flash.
- Images: none on hover; a caption may fade in 200 ms.
- Sticky: a slim section label or byline rail may pin; nothing else.
- No scroll reveals, no parallax, no transforms on type.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce → disable transforms, keep opacity.
